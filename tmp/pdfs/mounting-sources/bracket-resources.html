These aluminum gearmotor brackets let you securely mount Pololu’s 25D mm metal gearmotors to your project. The brackets are sold in pairs, and each bracket includes two M3 screws for securing the motor to the bracket. Each bracket also features seven mounting holes and twelve mounting slots for M3 or #4-size screws (not included), giving you a variety of mounting options.
